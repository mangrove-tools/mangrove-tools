What Mangrove Tools is

Mangrove Tools is not a dashboard, course portal, or “AI wrapper” brand. It is a static site of single-purpose utilities: pick a tool, get one clear answer, leave when you’re done. No accounts. No hard paywall on calculator results. Soft affiliate links may appear after a tool has been useful.

Creator tools

Free newsletter monetization helpers running entirely in your browser:

  • LetterROI — newsletter revenue calculator
  • SponsorQuote — sponsorship rate calculator
  • SubTarget — paid subscriber goal calculator
  • Media Kit Composer — print-ready media kit
  • Inventory Planner — sponsorship capacity over time

Analytics for small businesses

The same commitment to clarity extends to marketing analytics: budget optimization, marginality analysis, and revenue forecasting — simplified for small businesses and founders without a data analyst. All calculations run client-side; nothing leaves your browser.

  • Budget Advisor — marginal ROI and optimized allocation
  • Revenue Forecaster — trend, seasonality, and scenarios
  • Analytics hub — all analytics tools

Naples, Florida

Mangrove Tools is built in Naples on Florida’s Gulf Coast. The brand preference is calm, specific, and useful — Fraunces and Outfit, paper and pine — over generic AI purple gradients and “make money online” theatrics.

Who’s behind Mangrove Tools

Mangrove Tools is built and maintained by an independent maker on Florida’s Gulf Coast. It’s a small, deliberately un-venture-backed project: real utilities, honest math, no growth-hacking theatrics.
