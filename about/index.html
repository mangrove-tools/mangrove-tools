What Mangrove Tools is

Mangrove Tools is a free, in-browser marketing analytics suite for small businesses. The primary tools help with budget optimization, marginality analysis, and revenue forecasting. Supporting newsletter calculators help creators answer monetization questions quickly. No accounts. No hard paywall on results. Soft affiliate links may appear after a newsletter calculator has been useful.

Analytics for small businesses

Marketing analytics comes first: simplified, browser-based models for founders and marketers without a data analyst. All calculations run client-side; nothing leaves your browser.

  • Budget Advisor — marginal ROI and optimized allocation
  • Revenue Forecaster — trend, seasonality, and scenarios
  • Analytics hub — all analytics tools

Newsletter calculators

Supporting newsletter monetization helpers running entirely in your browser:

  • LetterROI — newsletter revenue calculator
  • SponsorQuote — sponsorship rate calculator
  • SubTarget — paid subscriber goal calculator
  • Media Kit Composer — print-ready media kit
  • Inventory Planner — sponsorship capacity over time

Naples, Florida

Mangrove Tools is built in Naples on Florida’s Gulf Coast. The brand preference is calm, specific, and useful — Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Sans, paper and pine — over generic AI purple gradients and “make money online” theatrics.

Who’s behind Mangrove Tools

Mangrove Tools is built and maintained by an independent maker on Florida’s Gulf Coast. It’s a small, deliberately un-venture-backed project: real utilities, honest math, no growth-hacking theatrics.
