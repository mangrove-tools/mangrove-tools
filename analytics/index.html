How the analytics tools work

Data stays on your device

All calculations run client-side in your browser. Your spend data, conversion numbers, and revenue history never leave your machine. No accounts required.

Honest about limitations

These are simplified models — not a full marketing mix modeling platform. They use statistical approximations that work well for small businesses with limited historical data. Every result includes a confidence indicator and clear guardrails.

Guided by real methodology

The math is based on the same concepts agencies use: power-law response curves, marginal efficiency analysis, and time-series decomposition — but stripped of complexity and delivered through plain-English interfaces.

Who these tools are for

Founders managing their own marketing

You have a Google Ads account, a Meta budget, maybe an email list or organic content. You want to know where to put the next dollar — not hire a consultant to figure it out.

Small marketing teams without a data analyst

Your team is two people wearing many hats. You have data in spreadsheets but no BI tool and no budget for an agency retainer. These tools bridge the gap.
