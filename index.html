How the numbers work

About

Statistical, not made up

The analytics tools use real response-curve fitting and time-series decomposition — power-law curves and seasonal indices, not vibes. See the methodology on each tool page.

Your data stays put

Every calculation runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server. Privacy.
