Methodology · how the numbers are made

How the Mangrove calculators work

Every estimate combines the numbers you enter with directional creator-economy benchmarks. Here is exactly what those benchmarks are, how each tool uses them, and — just as important — what they can’t tell you.

What these numbers are (and are not)

The calculators are planning tools, not predictions. They translate audience inputs into a reasonable monthly range using published-order-of-magnitude benchmarks that are common in the creator-economy and newsletter-sponsorship space. They are deliberately conservative and directional.

  • They are: a fast sanity check, a starting rate card, and a way to compare scenarios before you invest in growth.
  • They are not: a guarantee, a quote from any specific ad network or sponsor, or a substitute for your own rate testing. Real results depend on list quality, niche, offer, and sales process.

Niche benchmarks

All three tools share the same niche assumptions. Values are per-niche midpoints; the tools widen them into a low–high band (see each formula below).

Niche Ad RPM (per 1k opens) Sponsorship CPM Paid conversion
Finance / investing $18 $45 2.5%
Tech / startups $14 $35 2.0%
Health / wellness $11 $28 1.8%
Lifestyle / culture $9 $22 1.5%
General interest $7 $18 1.2%

Where these come from: they are compiled from publicly discussed ranges for newsletter ad RPM, direct-sponsorship CPM, and free-to-paid conversion, then rounded conservatively so the output leans cautious rather than optimistic. Higher-intent niches (finance, tech) command higher CPMs; broad general-interest lists command less. Treat them as order-of-magnitude, not gospel — and override them with your own rates when you have them (SponsorQuote supports a custom CPM).

LetterROI — revenue range

LetterROI estimates monthly revenue from three streams and sums them:

  • Ads: monthly opens ÷ 1,000 × niche ad RPM. Monthly opens = subscribers × open rate × emails per month.
  • Sponsorships: roughly one primary sponsor slot every two sends, priced per-send at the niche sponsorship CPM.
  • Paid subscriptions: subscribers × niche paid conversion × your monthly price.

The three midpoints are summed, then spread into a low–high band (streams are weighted down for the low end and up for the high end) to reflect real-world variance. The midpoint you see in the note is the un-widened sum.

SponsorQuote — one sponsorship slot

SponsorQuote prices a single primary placement: estimated opens ÷ 1,000 × niche CPM, adjusted by placement type (exclusive sponsor commands a premium; multi-sponsor issues share attention and price lower). It reports a flat fee, the implied CPM, and a low–high band around the quote.

SubTarget — paid subscribers needed

SubTarget works backward from a revenue goal. It computes net revenue per paying subscriber (your price minus the platform fee, blended with any annual-plan mix), then divides your target by that net to find the subscribers needed, with a ±15% band and an optional churn-replacement figure.

Why a range instead of one number

A single figure implies false precision. Sponsorship rates, conversion, and fill vary widely between two lists of identical size. A band is honest about that uncertainty and is more useful for planning: aim for the middle, pressure-test the low end.

Every calculation runs in your browser. The numbers you type are never sent to a Mangrove Tools server. See the privacy page for details.
