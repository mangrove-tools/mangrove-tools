Privacy

Your marketing data stays with you

Mangrove Tools is built as static pages. Marketing analytics tools and supporting newsletter calculators run entirely in your browser.

What we do not collect from tools

Budget Advisor, Revenue Forecaster, LetterROI, SponsorQuote, SubTarget, and other in-browser tools do not POST your inputs to Mangrove Tools servers. Values you type are used locally to compute results on the page.

Hosting and logs

Like most websites, our host may process standard request logs (for example IP address, user agent, and requested URL) to serve pages and maintain the site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (Google tag G-E20401V5WB) to understand aggregate traffic and page use. Google may process data such as IP address, device/browser information, and pages visited under Google’s privacy policy. Marketing analytics and newsletter calculator inputs you type are still not POSTed to Mangrove Tools servers as part of the tool math — they stay in your browser for on-page calculation.

Affiliate links

Some supporting newsletter calculator pages include affiliate links (for example to newsletter platforms). Those links may include tracking parameters so a partner can attribute a referral. Affiliate relationships are disclosed with sponsored link attributes where used. Clicking an affiliate link takes you to a third-party site with its own privacy policy.

Analytics tools

The analytics tools (Budget Advisor and Revenue Forecaster) process your marketing data entirely in your browser. No spend, conversion, or revenue data is transmitted to Mangrove Tools servers. See individual tool pages for methodology notes.
