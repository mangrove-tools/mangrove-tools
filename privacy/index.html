Privacy

Your calculator inputs stay with you

Mangrove Tools is built as static pages. Core calculators run entirely in your browser.

What we do not collect from tools

LetterROI, SponsorQuote, and other in-browser calculators do not POST your inputs to Mangrove Tools servers. Values you type are used locally to compute results on the page.

Hosting and logs

Like most websites, our host may process standard request logs (for example IP address, user agent, and requested URL) to serve pages and maintain the site. We do not run third-party analytics SDKs on the core tool experience unless that changes with an explicit product decision and disclosure update.

Affiliate links

Some tool pages include affiliate links (for example to newsletter platforms). Those links may include tracking parameters so a partner can attribute a referral. Affiliate relationships are disclosed with sponsored link attributes where used. Clicking an affiliate link takes you to a third-party site with its own privacy policy.

Lead-Dev product pages

Checkout, booking, or waitlist links on Lead-Dev pages — when configured — are provided by third-party vendors chosen by the site owner. Until those links are live, buttons remain placeholders and do not send payment or personal data.
