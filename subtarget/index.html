How the subscriber goal estimate works

SubTarget is the inverse of LetterROI’s paid stream. You set a monthly revenue goal and subscription economics — not list size or conversion benchmarks:

  • Net per sub — monthly price × (1 − platform fee%), blended with annual plan share when provided.
  • Subscribers needed — revenue goal ÷ net per sub, rounded up.
  • Planning band — ±15% around the point estimate for conservative vs stretch planning.
  • Churn replacements — optional steady-state new subs per month to offset monthly churn.

Figures are directional planning math, not guarantees. Conversion, upgrades, refunds, and churn vary by niche and offer.

Paid subscriber goal FAQ

How many paid newsletter subscribers do I need?

Divide your monthly revenue goal by net revenue per paying subscriber after platform fees. SubTarget rounds up and shows a ±15% band so you can plan conservatively or stretch.

How does SubTarget differ from LetterROI?

LetterROI projects forward revenue from list size, open rate, and niche benchmarks. SubTarget starts with your paid revenue goal and price, then tells you how many paying subscribers that requires.

Are SubTarget subscriber counts guaranteed?

No. The model uses your inputs and simple fee math. Treat the output as planning guidance, not a promise you will convert or retain that many subscribers.
